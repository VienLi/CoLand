The `diagrams/en/` and `diagrams/zh-CN/` folders contain the English and Chinese SVG illustrations embedded in the corresponding website articles:

- **Judgments About the Future:** `model-cost-outlook`, `human-attention-bottleneck`, `decision-delegation`, `agent-majority`, and `portable-judgment`.
- **Giving People Their Attention Back:** `feedback-routing`.
- **Decisions Are the Most Important Asset:** `judgment-evidence`, `personal-decision-assets`, `decision-review`, and `judgment-model-routing`.

The three Mermaid diagrams in Giving People Their Attention Back are adaptations of the website's published flow diagrams, with a source link beside each diagram. They illustrate the same relationships without requiring website components. Illustrations may show proposed workflows; they are not a separate list of released features.
