## Sources and synchronization

The [Chinese originals](https://www.coland.ai/zh/articles) and [English editions](https://www.coland.ai/articles) remain on the website. This repository holds selected copies for reading and discussion on GitHub; the full article collection stays on the website. Last synchronized: **2026-11-14**.

Each article records its original publication date, source update date, and synchronization date. We preserve the distinction between available capabilities and future ideas. See the [FAQ](../FAQ.md) for current integrations and downloads.

Covers and SVG illustrations come from the website. Three diagrams that rely on website components are adapted as GitHub-supported Mermaid diagrams, with links to the original figures. The full article text and paragraph order are retained, with local links between installments.

Updates are synchronized manually: substantive Chinese content changes go to the website first, followed by the repository copy and English translation. Translation and formatting fixes do not change source dates.
